**Positive.** Deception among interacting AI agents is a safety-relevant and under-measured
behavior. We contribute an instrument, a released trace dataset, and metrics that enable
measurement rather than anecdote, plus evidence practitioners can act on when selecting
models for multi-agent deployments.

**Risks and mitigations.** The environment elicits deceptive behavior by design; it is a
*measurement* instrument, not a training or optimization target, and we release traces and
metrics rather than tuned deceptive policies. Model-level results are directional and
single-seed and should **not** be used for procurement or marketing claims — stated in
Limitations and in the released dataset card.

**Release.** Environment, traces, judge prompts, and analysis code are released for
